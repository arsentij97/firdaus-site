The short version

Do I have to leave Instagram or TikTok?

No. And you don’t need to film anything new: post the same videos with no music you already have. On Firdaus they reach people who came for exactly this.

What does it cost?

Creators in the program get Premium for free. For everyone else posting videos needs a Plus subscription; watching Reminders is free.

When do I start earning?

The Partner Program works from launch: 20% of subscriptions from people you bring. Brand deals come later, and we don’t promise dates.

How many people are on Firdaus already?

The app launches in late November 2026, inshaAllah. Before launch there are no viewers. That is why we are inviting you now: the first creators are seen first.

How many followers do I need?

As a rough guide for the Creator Program, around 10,000. But we choose the first creators by hand and look at the work itself. If we wrote to you ourselves, we already like your work.

Which language should I post in?

Your own. When posting you mark the video language and the feed takes it to people who will understand. Quran recitation is shown to everyone.
