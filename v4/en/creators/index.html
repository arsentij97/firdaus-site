How their feeds work

The Instagram and TikTok algorithm doesn’t work for you. It works for time spent in the app.

You know this from the inside. So do we, which is why we built something else.

  1. An endless feed on dopamine

    The feed never ends, by design. Every swipe is a small reward, and the brain waits for the next one. The longer people scroll, the more ads they see: that is their business.

  2. Music fuels the algorithm

    A trending sound pushes a video into recommendations. You leave music out on principle, and you know how that ends: reach drops and the video sinks.

  3. Your viewer scrolls into junk

    Someone watches your video to the end, says “SubhanAllah”, and a second later scrolls on: a prank, a dance, a stranger’s life. Finding you there again is like looking for treasure in a landfill.
