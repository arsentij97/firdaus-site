Common questions

How do I find accurate prayer times for my city?

Firdaus calculates prayer times for your city. In Kazakhstan, times come from the DUMK timetable, in Tatarstan from the DUM RT timetable, in Türkiye from Diyanet; elsewhere, from the calculation method and madhhab you chose. After the first download, the timetable works offline too.

How do I find the qibla direction?

Firdaus has a compass with the Kaaba marked on it and a map with a line to Mecca. Keep your phone level and away from metal and magnets: near them, a compass goes wrong.

Is Firdaus free?

Prayer times, the qibla, the prayer reminder, marking a prayer, the Quran with one translation and the tasbih are always free. Firdaus Plus opens prayer history, loved ones, Umma, tahajjud and Focus; the first week is free, then $9.99 a month or $59.99 a year in the US.

How do I remind a loved one to pray?

Add a brother, sister or friend to your loved ones. When you can see they haven’t prayed yet, send a card with one tap: an ayah or a warm word. If they don’t have Firdaus yet, the card goes as a link in any messenger.

Will Firdaus help in Ramadan?

Yes. Fajr time shows when suhoor ends, and Maghrib time shows when it’s iftar. Reminders, tahajjud in the last third of the night, the Quran and a shared dhikr count help you keep going all month.

Is Firdaus on Android?

Right now Firdaus runs on iPhone with iOS 17 or later. An Android version comes next.
