6. Religious texts and prayer times

  • We are not scholars. Firdaus issues no fatwas and is no substitute for a person of knowledge. Take a question of faith to an imam or scholar you trust.
  • Quran. The Arabic text is from the Tanzil Project (tanzil.net). Translations come from QuranEnc.com: Russian by Rowwad Translation Center, English by Saheeh International (Noor International Center); other languages are from the same source. A translation conveys meaning and is not the Quran itself. Sources and licences are listed in the app: Quran → “Sources and licenses”.
  • Dhikr and dua. Each text in the app names its source.
  • Prayer times. Calculated for your location with the method you chose, or taken from the timetable of your country’s official authority. They may differ from your mosque’s timetable by a few minutes. When in doubt, check with your mosque; the calculation method and madhhab can be changed in settings.
  • Qibla. The direction depends on your phone’s sensors; a compass is thrown off near metal and magnets.
  • Prayer check and streak are tools for self-discipline. They say nothing about whether your prayer is accepted and promise no reward: only Allah knows that.

Found a mistake in a text or translation? Write to support and we will fix it.

  • You can always get out. There is a pause and an emergency exit, and you can withdraw the permission in iPhone settings.
  • Don’t close what you need. Don’t pick apps that your health, safety, work or contact with family depend on. What you use and what you close is your decision and your responsibility.
  • No guarantee. Focus depends on iOS: after a system update, a withdrawn permission or a fault, apps may not close or open on time.
  • Reminders. Prayer notifications, cards and the “together” alarm are delivered by iOS and Apple’s servers. They can be late or not arrive at all — because of Do Not Disturb, no connection or system limits. Don’t make them your only alarm.
