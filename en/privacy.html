Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026. Applies to the Firdaus app (iPhone) and the firdaus.life website.

In short

  • Prayer check photos, your cycle calendar and your coordinates never leave your phone.
  • Our server holds what your loved ones need to see you: profile, prayer marks, posts, messages.
  • We don’t sell data, show ads or track you across other apps.
  • You delete your account in the app: Settings → “Delete account”. Your data and files go with it.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is the developer of Firdaus, an individual based in Kazakhstan. The name is shown as “Seller” on the Firdaus page in the App Store. You can reach us on Telegram at @firdaus_support or by email at support@firdaus.life.

2. What stays on your phone

We never receive any of this — not on the server, not in analytics, not in crash reports.

  • Prayer check photos. When you confirm a prayer with the camera, the frame is analysed on your phone and is not saved. Only the mark “verified” leaves the device.
  • Coordinates. Location is used to calculate prayer times and the qibla direction. Your phone does the maths. Neither coordinates nor your city are sent to our server. You can decline location access and pick a city from the list.
  • Cycle calendar. Stored only on your phone. Loved ones can see neither the calendar nor the fact that you keep one.
  • The list of apps for Focus. You choose them in Apple’s system picker. iPhone does not tell us which apps you chose.
  • Settings and drafts. Calculation method, madhhab, theme, unfinished posts and messages.
  • Using Firdaus without an account. Until you sign in, everything you do — marks, streak, settings — lives only on your phone.

Two caveats about location, to be straight with you:

  • To name your city, your phone may ask Apple’s geocoding service. That is done by iOS, not by our server.
  • If “Official timetable” is on, your phone downloads the prayer timetable for the nearest city from the servers that publish the timetables of official religious authorities (the Muslim boards of Kazakhstan and Tatarstan, Türkiye’s Directorate of Religious Affairs). The request names a city from our list, not your exact position. Like any website, that server sees your phone’s IP address.

4. Why we need it

  • To make the app work — sign-in, loved ones, cards, chat, Umma, subscription. Without this data those features don’t exist.
  • To keep Umma clean — an automated check of posts, photos, Instants, names and avatars before they are published (done by OpenAI, only with your consent in the app) and handling reports.
  • To fix what breaks — crash reports.
  • To understand what works — de-identified events: opened the app, finished onboarding, marked a first prayer, added a loved one.

You grant access to the camera, microphone, photos, location, notifications and Screen Time yourself in iPhone’s system prompts, and you can take it back at any time in your phone’s settings. The camera runs only on the prayer check screen and when you take a photo for a post; the microphone only while you hold the voice message button.

We don’t use data for advertising, don’t build an advertising profile of you and don’t sell data to anyone.

6. What other people see

  • Everyone on Firdaus: your username, name, avatar and the posts you published to “Everyone”.
  • Loved ones and your circle: whether you have prayed the current prayer, your streak and missed prayers — unless you hid them in privacy settings; when you were last online.
  • The person you chat with: the messages you sent them.
  • No one: your cycle calendar, prayer check photos, your coordinates.

The app does not fill in a city on your profile and does not show one to others.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account and everything tied to it — until you delete the account.
  • Stories — 24 hours, then deleted together with their files. Highlights pinned to your profile stay until you remove them.
  • Deleted messages, photos and voice messages — files are erased from the server within a few hours.
  • Database backups — overwritten within 30 days at most.
  • Crash reports — up to 90 days.
  • Usage events — up to 12 months.
  • Reports and decisions on them — for as long as needed to resolve them and keep offenders out.
  • Purchase records — for as long as accounting and tax law requires.

8. Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → “Delete account”. Deletion is permanent. It erases your profile, prayer marks, loved ones and circles, posts, replies, stories, messages and files — avatar, photos, voice messages. If you signed in with Apple, we revoke Firdaus’s access to your Apple ID.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription — that is done in your Apple ID settings (see the Terms).

Can’t get into the app? Write to support and we will delete the account on request.

9. Your rights

You can:

  • find out what data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct your data — most of it right in the app;
  • delete your account and data;
  • object to processing or ask us to restrict it;
  • withdraw permissions in iPhone settings.

Write to @firdaus_support or support@firdaus.life — we reply within 30 days. If you believe we have violated your rights, you may contact the data protection authority in your country.

10. Children

Firdaus is not intended for children under 13. If the law in your country sets a higher age for consenting to data processing on your own (up to 16 in EU countries), that age applies. We do not knowingly collect children’s data. If you are a parent and see that your child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

11. How we protect data

  • The connection to the server is encrypted.
  • Every table in the database is locked down by access rules: a person sees only their own data and what others have opened to them.
  • Files are kept in private storage.
  • Sign-in keys are kept in iPhone’s secure storage.

No protection is absolute. If a breach ever affects you, we will tell you.

12. Changes

When this policy changes, we update the date at the top of the page. We will warn you in the app ahead of any significant change.
